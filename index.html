Instructions

A little care keeps every stitch soft and every face smiling.

Washing

  1. Step 1: Fill a bowl with cool to lukewarm water and add a tiny drop of gentle detergent. Avoid fabric softeners.

  2. Step 2: Submerge the amigurumi and gently press or swish it in the water. For stubborn spots, use soft circular motions with a cloth, but do not scrub hard, twist, or wring.

  3. Step 3: Rinse thoroughly with clean, cool running water until all soap residue is gone.

Drying

  1. Step 1: Press the amigurumi gently, then wrap it tightly in a clean, dry towel and press down. Do not twist or wring the toy.

  2. Step 2: Reshape the toy with your hands while damp and lay it flat on a dry towel or drying rack in a shaded, well-ventilated area. Turn it over occasionally. Avoid direct sunlight or hanging it by its ears or limbs, which stretches the shape.
